The Math Does Not Work

A team of 2.5 people cannot simultaneously serve an existing client book at full capacity AND absorb 186 integration asks from 31 different NFP contacts across 9 functional categories. The email volume alone represents 10.9 hours of reading time ... that is before a single reply, phone call, or form submission. The close-week spike of 13.4 emails per day from NFP is the new normal, not an anomaly. Either the integration workload needs centralized coordination, or the team needs capacity relief. Ideally both.
